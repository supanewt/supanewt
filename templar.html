Templar

Frontline · Support

An armored warrior sent to contain the plague and investigate its source. The Templar stands between enemies and weaker party members, using armor, shields, positioning, and leadership to keep the party alive.

Overview

Templar is a class, not a fixed kit. Every recruit who carries it is built the same way every other mercenary in the archive is: four attributes rolled at random from the class's pool, one random passive gained every four levels, and no way back once a character falls. Two Templars standing side by side can play nothing alike—one might anchor the front line with a shield and a mace, another might hang back with a crossbow and a banner of leadership.

What every Templar shares is the role: they are built to be hard to kill and harder to get past, trading speed and magic for defense, courage, and the ability to keep everyone else alive.

Proficiencies

Swordsman

Proficiency with one- and two-handed swords. Deepens into cutting and finesse-focused techniques.

Axebearer

Proficiency with one- and two-handed axes. Deepens into cleaving and momentum-focused techniques.

Maceman

Proficiency with maces. Deepens into crushing and stagger-focused techniques.

Mauler

Proficiency with hammers and great hammers. Deepens into crushing and stagger-focused techniques.

Marksman

Proficiency with bows and crossbows. Deepens into precision, mobility, and high-damage ranged techniques.

Shieldbearer

Proficiency with shields. Required to get real use out of one—unlocks blocking, bashing, and ally-protection techniques.

Medium Armor

Lets the Templar wear medium armor without a speed penalty, and deepens its defensive value.

Heavy Armor

Lets the Templar wear heavy armor without a full speed penalty, and deepens its defensive value.

Leadership

Strengthens the Templar's effect on the whole party, boosting party-wide buffs and support effects.

Protection

Strengthens the Templar's ability to shield a specific ally, boosting single-target defensive techniques.

Courage

Strengthens resistance to fear and courage-based effects.

Purification

Strengthens effectiveness against supernatural and infected enemies.

Endurance

Strengthens health and stamina, and resistance to exhaustion in long fights.

Disease Resistance

Strengthens resistance to plague effects, infection, and prolonged miasma exposure.

Starting Abilities

Basic Attack

Attack using the equipped weapon.

Guard

Braces for an attack and receives reduced damage until the Templar's next turn. With a shield equipped, Guard also reduces the chance of suffering an injury.

Shield Bash

Strikes an adjacent enemy with the equipped shield. Low damage, but may push, stagger, or interrupt the target.

Special Abilities

Second Wind

Passive. Once per battle, the Templar's Stamina is restored to maximum.

Spirit—the resource every class draws magic from—is only restored by rest or consumables. Stamina is the one reserve a Templar can call back mid-fight.

Exploration Abilities

Military Training

The Templar can recognize defensive positions, weak barricades, weapon damage, signs of combat, guard formations, military insignia, properly maintained equipment, likely ambush locations, and structural positions that can be defended.

May unlock additional dialogue with guards, soldiers, mercenaries, and nobles.

Basic Authority

The Templar may attempt to question guards, request access to restricted areas, examine official records, order civilians away from danger, speak with military commanders, or inspect quarantined buildings.

People aren't required to obey. Some residents fear the Templar; others believe the Templar was sent to kill everyone.
