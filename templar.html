Class Record 01

Templar

Not every soldier walks into the miasma with a blade in one hand and a prayer in the other. The Templar does. Ordained by an order that stopped waiting for miracles and started making them, they carry both the steel to hold a line and the faith to mend what's broken behind it—the closest thing this dying town has left to a saint who still remembers how to fight. Every recruit who takes up the name is built the same way every other mercenary in the archive is: four skill sets rolled at random, one passive gained every four levels, and no second chances once they fall. Two Templars can still look nothing alike—one anchors the front line behind a shield and a mace, muttering old rites between blows; another hangs back with a crossbow and a banner of leadership; a third channels Holy Magic to mend the wounded from behind the line, hands glowing faintly gold in the dark. What every Templar shares isn't a weapon or a spell—it's the promise. Hard to kill, harder to get past, and impossible to leave behind: they hold the line so the ones without armor or magic of their own don't have to.
