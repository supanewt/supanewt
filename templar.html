Templar

Frontline · Support

An armored warrior sent to contain the plague and investigate its source. The Templar stands between enemies and weaker party members, using armor, shields, positioning, leadership, and holy magic to keep the party alive.

Overview

Templar is a class, not a fixed kit. Every recruit who carries it is built the same way every other mercenary in the archive is: four attributes rolled at random from the class's pool, one random passive gained every four levels, and no way back once a character falls. Two Templars standing side by side can play nothing alike—one might anchor the front line with a shield and a mace, another might hang back with a crossbow and a banner of leadership, and another might channel Holy Magic to mend the wounded from behind the line.

What every Templar shares is the role: they are built to be hard to kill and harder to get past, drawing on holy magic, unshakeable defense, and courage to keep everyone else alive.
