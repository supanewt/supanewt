Sir Aldren Vey

A plague hunter sent to decide whether the castle town can be saved—or whether everything living within it must burn.

Overview

Aldren enters the miasma carrying sealed authority to burn infected districts, execute carriers, and destroy every road out of town. He believes hesitation can kill thousands. He also knows fear can turn righteous men into monsters.

He has until the thirtieth day to find and destroy the source before duty demands that he condemn everyone still alive.

“Mercy must save more than the person receiving it.”

Classified Truth · The Relic Knows Him

The reliquary Aldren believes is holy contains a fragment of the same demon beneath the town. He has unknowingly carried the plague between settlements for years.

If Aldren Is Not Selected

Aldren is not recruitable if he isn't chosen as the starting protagonist, and his arrival is not guaranteed—it's decided by RNG, and in some playthroughs he never shows up at all. When he does appear, he arrives on Day 24 with two Templars and begins killing to contain the plague. From that point on he only adds new story events; he cannot be persuaded to join the company.

Recruitment

Unlike other archive characters, Aldren has no recruitment path. He is either chosen as the starting protagonist before Day 1, or encountered later purely as a story event—he cannot be persuaded, bargained with, or maneuvered into joining the company by any means. He arrives with two other Templars, and all three can be engaged in battle and killed.

Combat Identity

Aldren is an armored frontline Templar who intercepts attacks, identifies plague threats, and turns defensive pressure into ruthless counterattacks.

Signature Skill · Interpose

Move into the path of an attack targeting an adjacent ally. Aldren receives the strike and gains Resolve for surviving it.
